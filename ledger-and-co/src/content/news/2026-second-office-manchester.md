From this week the practice has two offices. The Manchester one opened on
Monday, on Booth Street, with four people in it — three from the London team
who asked to move, and one new hire who starts as a client manager in June.
Two more join in September.

## Why

Roughly a third of our clients are outside the South East, and about half of
those are within an hour of Manchester. We have been serving them from London
for six years, which has worked, in the sense that the accounts have been
filed and the advice has been good. It has been worse than it looks, though,
in the way that matters: a client who only ever sees their accountant over a
video call gets a different relationship from one whose accountant can be in
their warehouse on a Thursday afternoon.

There is a version of this business that scales by adding video calls. We are
not interested in building it. The work we do well — the advisory work, the
awkward transactions, the "should we buy this company" conversations — depends
on partners who have actually seen the operation they are advising on.

> An accountant who has walked the floor gives different advice from one who
> has read the management accounts. Both are honest. Only one is any use when
> the numbers and the reality disagree.

## What changes for clients

For most of you, nothing. Your engagement partner is the same person, your
manager is the same person, and the fee is the same fee. Nobody has been
reassigned as part of this and nobody will be without a conversation first.

Three practical differences:

- **Clients in the North West** now have a partner within driving distance,
  and we would like to use that. If your last three meetings have been video
  calls, expect one of us to propose coming to you instead.
- **Meeting rooms.** Booth Street has two, and they are available to clients
  who want somewhere neutral to hold a board meeting or a difficult
  conversation with a lender. Ask the front desk; there is no charge.
- **Audit fieldwork in the North** stops involving four people on a train at
  six in the morning, which will show up in your fees before it shows up
  anywhere else.

Our London office is unchanged and is not shrinking. The three people who
moved were replaced.
