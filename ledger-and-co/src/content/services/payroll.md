Payroll is the one service where being roughly right is worse than being late.
People notice their own pay to the penny, and an under-deduction found in month
nine is a conversation with an employee about money they have already spent.

We run a fixed timetable: your changes are due on a named day, we send a draft
gross-to-net report for approval, and nothing is filed until you have signed it
off. Payslips are issued through a portal your staff log into themselves, so HR
questions do not arrive in a director's inbox with a payslip attached.

Off-payroll workers, TUPE transfers and mid-year scheme migrations are all
handled in-house. If HMRC opens a PAYE compliance check, the person who ran the
payroll deals with it — you are not handed to a different department that has to
learn the file first.
