Advisory is the word firms use when they mean a slide deck. What we mean is
narrower and more useful: building the model that tells you whether the decision
you are about to make survives contact with your own cash position.

That usually starts with an uncomfortable month. Forecasts built by the people
who want the answer to be yes tend to have optimistic collection assumptions and
a headcount line that stops growing exactly when the revenue does not. We rebuild
it, show you the version where two things go wrong at once, and agree the point
at which you would change course.

After that it is a standing rhythm — a monthly reforecast against actuals, and a
partner in the room for the conversations where the number is contested. Not a
retainer for advice on request, but a seat at the decisions that move the
balance sheet.
