Most tax we see going wrong was not aggressive. It was late. A dividend voted
without checking distributable reserves, a property moved between two companies
in the same group, an option granted at a price nobody agreed with HMRC first —
each one is cheap to get right in advance and expensive to unpick afterwards.

We work to the tax year in front of you, not the one being filed. That means a
planning conversation in the autumn while there is still time to act, a written
note of the position we have taken and why, and the supporting analysis kept on
file — so if an enquiry lands in three years, the answer already exists.

Where a position is genuinely arguable, we tell you that it is arguable, what
the counter-argument would be, and what it would cost to defend. We do not sell
schemes, and we will say plainly when the tax-efficient answer is the wrong
answer commercially.
