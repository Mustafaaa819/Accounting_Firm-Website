Bookkeeping is where every other piece of work either starts clean or starts
badly. If the ledger is three months behind, the tax planning is guesswork, the
audit is an excavation, and the cash-flow forecast is a story told about numbers
nobody has checked.

So we run it as a monthly cycle with a fixed close date, not as a pile that gets
cleared before a deadline. Your transactions are coded and reconciled as they
land, queries come to you in one batch rather than in a fortnight of emails, and
the accounts are reviewed by a qualified accountant — not just exported — before
they reach you.

You keep the login to your own accounting system throughout. It is your data,
your subscription and your audit trail; we work inside it rather than holding
your records hostage in ours.
