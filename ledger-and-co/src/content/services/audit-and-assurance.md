We plan every engagement around the questions your board and your investors will
actually ask, then test the balances that carry the most risk of being wrong.
Revenue recognition, stock, work in progress and related-party transactions get
the hours. The petty cash count does not.

Engagements typically cover statutory year-end audit, group consolidation
reviews, and agreed-upon procedures ahead of a funding round. Fieldwork is
booked as a block with dates agreed in advance, so your finance team knows
exactly which fortnight it loses and gets the rest of the year back.

Independence cuts both ways. Where we audit, there is work we cannot also do for
you — and we will say so at the scoping stage rather than discovering it in
month two.
